/// Live company

A company that runs itself, and knows when to ask

Rimal Business Services is a Gulf B2B services firm staffed by four AI agents. They share one ledger, react to a live market, cover for each other when one fails, and send a human only the decisions they are not allowed to make.

Adaptive company Static baseline: same market and roles, fixed playbook, no signals between roles, no human inbox

/// Human inbox

Only what the agents may not decide

/// Agents

Four roles, one ledger

Switch one off to run the failure test. The watchdog notices after two missed heartbeats.

/// Event stream

Everything that just happened

    /// Adaptive vs. baseline

    Same market, same luck, different company

    Both companies get identical leads, payments and failures from one seed. The baseline follows a fixed playbook. The adaptive one listens to its own signals.

    /// Shared records

    The ledger every agent reads and writes

    /// Replay

    Rebuild any day from the log

    State is never edited directly. It is a fold of an append-only event log in SQLite, so any closed day can be rebuilt and checked against the hash recorded live.

    /// Architecture

    How the company is wired

    Market streamLeads, payments, tickets, demand spikes. Seeded, so both companies face the same world.
    →
    Event busEvery change is an event. Each agent has a durable mailbox, so nothing is lost while it is down.
    →
    Shared ledgerAppend-only log in SQLite. Records are a fold of the log: leads, customers, jobs, invoices, tickets.
    SalesQualifies, prices, closes. Throttles when Ops is full.
    OpsDelivers jobs. Raises capacity alerts, asks for budget.
    FinanceInvoices, chases, approves spend, guards runway.
    SupportTriages tickets, retains unhappy customers.
    WatchdogCounts heartbeats. Tells every agent when a colleague goes silent.
    →
    Human inboxDecisions above an agent's authority. Unanswered items take a safe default.
    →
    ReplayFold the log to any day and compare hashes.

    Escalation rules

    Where a human is still needed

    • Pricing exceptions. Deep discounts on large retainers trade margin for growth. That is a strategy call.
    • Writing off debt. Finance never forgives money owed on its own.
    • Spend beyond authority. Anything above 15% of cash, or with a short runway.
    • Restarting a failed role. The agents cover; a person decides whether to bring it back.
    • Everything outside the model. Hiring, contracts, regulators and relationships are not simulated here, and would stay human.
